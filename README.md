# Liftlog

A workout tracker for iPhone that runs as a web app.

**Open it:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/liftlog/

**Install on iPhone:** open the link in Safari → tap the Share button → *Add to Home Screen*. It then opens full screen like a normal app and works offline.

Your workouts, routines and body weight are saved on your phone (in the browser storage of the Home Screen app). They are not uploaded anywhere.
